# Gulf Bite

Fishing and boating forecast for your own spots: 7-day fishing score, bite chance by hour, best times, NOAA tides, wind, waves, water temperature, pressure (inHg), and a catch log that remembers the conditions for every fish.

Open it at https://williecdalmau-hub.github.io/gulf-bite/ and use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Data: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) weather and marine forecasts, [NOAA CO-OPS](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov) tide predictions, sun and moon calculated on the phone with SunCalc. Map imagery © Esri.
